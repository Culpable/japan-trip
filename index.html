    04

    Buy your MTR ticket

    One paid journey covers all three trains and two transfers

    At Lok Ma Chau Station, buy one single-journey ticket per person.

    1. Press English.
    2. Select Tung Chung as the destination.
    3. Pay the displayed fare.
    4. Keep the ticket throughout the journey.
    5. Insert or scan it at the entry gate.

    One ticket covers the complete journey and both transfers if you remain inside the paid area.

    You can instead use an Octopus card or a separate contactless bank card for each passenger. Use a designated contactless gate. MTR accepts contactless credit or debit cards, except UnionPay debit cards.
